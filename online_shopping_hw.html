Part 1: Formal Register & Customer Correspondence Drill
Task 1.1: Formal Commercial Synonyms
Замени разговорные глаголы в скобках на официальные коммерческие соответствия: [receive, request, provide, refund, assistance, apologise, inform, be delighted]
  1. I would like to ___________ [tell] you that the item is currently out of stock. →
  2. We will be ___________ [happy] to dispatch your order early tomorrow morning. →
  3. Please ___________ [give] your transaction ID and the credit card receipt. →
  4. I am writing to ___________ [say sorry] for the difficulties with your delivery. →
  5. Could you please ___________ [give back money] the full sum to my bank account? →
  6. Thank you for contacting customer support; we are happy to offer any ___________ [help]. →
Task 1.2: Customer Helpline Dialogue Expressions
Вставь подходящее слово в телефонный диалог службы поддержки: [hold, confirm, line, stock, replacement]
  1. Assistant: "Could you please ___________ your delivery address and postal code?" →
  2. Assistant: "Can I put you on ___________ for one moment while I check the warehouse?" →
  3. Customer: "If the hard-drive clock is out of ___________, would you prefer to cancel it?" →
  4. Customer: "I do not want to cancel the order; I would rather receive a ___________." →
  5. Assistant: "Thank you for staying on the ___________; your new invoice has been emailed." →
Part 2: Grammar Workout (Polite Requests & Indirect Questions)
Task 2.1: Transforming Blunt Demands into Polite Requests
Перепиши резкие фразы вежливо с использованием Could you please... или I would like to...:
  1. Direct: "Give me the invoice right now."
    Polite: Could you please ?
  2. Direct: "Cancel my order immediately."
    Polite: I would like to .
  3. Direct: "Tell me your order reference number."
    Polite: Could you please ?
Task 2.2: Indirect Question Word Order
Составь вежливый непрямой вопрос (помни: порядок слов как в утверждении — Subject + Verb):
  1. Direct: When will my parcel arrive?
    Indirect: Could you please tell me when ?
  2. Direct: Why is the payment delayed?
    Indirect: Do you know why the payment ?
  3. Direct: Can I receive a refund to my credit card?
    Indirect: I would like to enquire whether I .
Task 2.3: Spot and Correct the Mistake
В каждом деловом предложении ровно одна ошибка. Напиши исправленное предложение целиком:
  1. Could you tell me where is the package right now? →
  2. I would be grateful if you can refund my money. →
Part 3: Formal Writing (Customer Complaint Email)
Task 3.1: Drafting a Formal Complaint
Представь, что ты заказала в интернет-магазине Tecoart настенные часы из переработанных деталей компьютера (Order No: 77412-B). Товар пришел с трещиной на стекле и не работает.
Напиши официальное письмо в службу поддержки (60–80 слов).
Обязательно включи: вежливое обращение (Dear Sir or Madam / Dear Customer Support), объяснение проблемы (I recently received...), номер заказа, вежливое требование замены или возврата денег (I would be grateful if you could refund...) и формальное прощание (Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully).
