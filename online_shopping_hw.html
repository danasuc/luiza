Part 2: Grammar Workout (Verb Patterns & Polite Modals)
Grammar Reminder:
• After like, you can use either to + infinitive (often preferred for habitual choices) or the -ing form (focusing on the activity/enjoyment): I like to track / I like tracking my parcels.
• After modal verbs such as can / could / would, always use the bare infinitive (without to): Can you help me? (NOT: Can you to help me).
